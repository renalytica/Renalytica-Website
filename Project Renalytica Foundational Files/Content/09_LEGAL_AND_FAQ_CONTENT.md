## 2. Understanding Licensing Options

### Q5: What is the difference between Single User, Departmental, and Enterprise licenses?
**A:** We keep our licensing rules straightforward:
* **Single User License ($2,500 standard):** For **one person** on one computer. You can read the report, but you cannot print it, share it with colleagues, or post it on company shared drives.
* **Departmental License ($4,500 standard):** For **up to 5 team members** in the same department. You get printable PDF rights, the unlocked Excel data spreadsheet, and a 1-hour briefing call with our research team.
* **Global Enterprise Site License ($7,500 standard):** For your **entire company**. Anyone in your organization—across all offices and branches worldwide—can access, read, and use the report. It includes all Excel models, presentation slides, and a 2-hour formal boardroom presentation by our senior economists.
