## 6. Quick Help FAQ (Right on the Contact Page)

* **Q: How quickly can you deliver a custom research project?**  
  *A:* Most custom research projects take between 4 to 8 weeks, depending on how deep the study needs to be. For urgent decisions, we can often deliver an initial high-level brief within 10 business days.
* **Q: Can you sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) before we share project details?**  
  *A:* Yes. We routinely sign mutual NDAs with corporate clients, investment funds, and advisory firms before reviewing proprietary project documents.
* **Q: How do I pay for reports or custom studies?**  
  *A:* We accept all major corporate credit cards, bank wire transfers (SWIFT), and formal company purchase orders (POs) for approved corporate accounts.
* **Q: Can I speak directly with the analyst who wrote a report before buying?**  
  *A:* Yes. Simply send us a message with the report title, and we will arrange a brief call or email exchange with our lead analyst to ensure the report covers exactly what you need.
* **Q: How does Renalytica collect and verify data for custom research projects?**  
  *A:* We use our institutional dual-track methodology: combining primary on-the-ground surveys, merchant price sampling, and port shipping logs with exhaustive aggregation of authoritative secondary resources (central bank bulletins, national statistics bureaus, multilateral databases, and audited corporate filings), all cross-checked through mathematical triangulation.
