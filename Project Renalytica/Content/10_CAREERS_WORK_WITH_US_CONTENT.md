## 7. How We Hire (Our 4-Step Process)

1. **1. Application Review (Within 5 Business Days):** We review your CV and writing/portfolio samples to assess mutual fit.
2. **2. Initial Discovery Conversation (30 Mins):** A friendly video call with our people team to discuss your background, interests, and what you are looking for in your next role.
3. **3. Practical Skills Exercise:** A short, real-world case study (e.g., analyzing a simple grain dataset or summarizing an economic trend) so you can experience the type of work we do.
4. **4. Team Discussion & Final Offer:** A conversation with our sector desk lead and founders to align on expectations, followed by a transparent, competitive offer.
