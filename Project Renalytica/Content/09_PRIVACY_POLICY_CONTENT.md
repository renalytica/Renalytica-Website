## 4. Section 2: Principles of Lawful Data Processing

In accordance with Section 24 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and Article 5 of the GDPR, all data processing undertaken by Renalytica adheres to the following foundational principles:
1. **Lawfulness, Fairness & Transparency:** Personal data is collected through fair, transparent means with explicit legal justifications.
2. **Purpose Limitation:** Data is obtained strictly for specified, explicit, and legitimate commercial intelligence and research fulfillment purposes.
3. **Data Minimization:** We collect only the minimum amount of data required to fulfill transactions, provide support, and deliver insights.
4. **Accuracy:** We maintain reasonable measures to ensure personal information remains accurate, current, and verified.
5. **Storage Limitation:** Information is retained only for as long as necessary to achieve the designated operational or statutory objectives.
6. **Integrity & Confidentiality:** Data is secured against unauthorized access, accidental loss, destruction, or compromise using rigorous technical and organizational controls.

## 5. Section 3: Categories of Personal Data We Collect

We collect information across five primary interaction categories:

### 3.1 Identification & Contact Data
* **When Collected:** When creating a user account, ordering a published report, requesting a sample brief, booking an executive briefing, or submitting an RFP.
* **Data Collected:** Full name, business email address, job title, corporate department, employer/company legal name, office telephone number, country of operation, and corporate WhatsApp number.

### 3.2 Transactional & Billing Information
* **When Collected:** When purchasing reports, upgrading licenses, or paying subscription invoices.
* **Data Collected:** Corporate billing address, invoice contact name, tax identification number (TIN / VAT ID), purchase history, license tier purchased, and payment method identifier.
* **Payment Card Security Note:** **Renalytica does NOT store raw credit or debit card numbers, CVVs, or bank security PINs on its servers.** All payment card transactions are tokenized and processed directly by our PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways (Stripe and Paystack).

### 3.3 Technical, Device & Telemetry Data
* **When Collected:** Automatically collected during your visits to `www.renalytica.com`.
* **Data Collected:** Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, operating system, device screen resolution, referring website URLs, geographic location (country and city level), time spent per page, and interaction heatmaps.

### 3.4 Communications & Support Inquiries
* **When Collected:** When you email our research desk, submit a custom inquiry, chat via WhatsApp, or engage during an analyst briefing call.
* **Data Collected:** Email correspondence history, meeting recordings (with prior attendee consent), custom project scope parameters, and feedback ratings.

### 3.5 Primary Fieldwork & Survey Data
* **When Collected:** During our primary economic research operations across agricultural and retail trade corridors.
* **Data Processing Standards:** All primary market surveys, enumerator interviews, and merchant price checks are **fully anonymized and aggregated** before publication. We never publish or expose the individual personal identity of surveyed traders, farmers, or logistics drivers.

## 10. Section 8: Information Security & Technical Safeguards

We implement institutional-grade technical, physical, and administrative safeguards to protect your personal and corporate information:

## 11. Section 9: Data Retention & Secure Destruction Schedules

11.1 **Retention Schedule:**
* **Purchased Report Download Records:** Retained for the active duration of your license plus three (3) years to facilitate license upgrades and file recovery requests.
* **Tax & Invoicing Documentation:** Retained for **seven (7) years** in accordance with statutory Nigerian corporate taxation and accounting audit laws.
* **Marketing & Newsletter Subscriptions:** Retained until you opt out or click "Unsubscribe". Upon unsubscribing, your email is immediately expunged from active mailing lists.
* **Technical Log Files:** Server access and error logs are automatically pruned and deleted after **ninety (90) days**.

11.2 **Secure Destruction:** Upon expiration of applicable retention periods, digital records are permanently overwritten using DoD-standard electronic shredding protocols.

## 12. Section 10: Your Legal Rights as a Data Subject

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you hold enforceable legal rights concerning your personal data:

1. **Right to Access (Subject Access Request):** You have the right to request a complete copy of all personal data Renalytica holds regarding you and your organization.
2. **Right to Rectification:** You have the right to demand the immediate correction of any inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated information.
3. **Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"):** You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your personal data, provided retention is no longer mandated by applicable financial or corporate taxation laws.
4. **Right to Restrict Processing:** You have the right to restrict our processing of your personal information under certain circumstances (e.g., while an accuracy dispute is being verified).
5. **Right to Data Portability:** You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format (e.g., JSON or CSV).
6. **Right to Object:** You have the right to object at any time to the processing of your personal data for direct marketing purposes.
7. **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Where processing is based on consent (such as our email newsletter), you may withdraw your consent instantly by clicking the unsubscribe link or contacting our DPO.

### How to Exercise Your Rights
To exercise any of these rights, submit a written request to our Data Protection Officer at `privacy@renalytica.com`. We will verify your identity and respond comprehensively within **thirty (30) calendar days** at zero cost to you.
