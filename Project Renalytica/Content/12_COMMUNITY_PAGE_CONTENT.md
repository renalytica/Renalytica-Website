## 3. Four Core Community Pillars

### 1. Researchers Link Ups (Professional Collaboration)
* **What It Is:** A collaborative network connecting independent economic researchers, academic scholars, and corporate data analysts.
* **Why Join:** Share on-the-ground field notes, discover co-authorship opportunities for upcoming industry reports, and exchange raw datasets.
* **Format:** Bi-weekly virtual working sessions and peer review circles.

### 2. Live Meetings & Roundtables (Virtual & Physical)
* **What It Is:** Structured, high-impact discussions on breaking market events, central bank policies, and commodity price shifts.
* **Format:**
  * **Virtual Roundtables (Zoom/Teams):** 60-minute interactive webinars featuring guest economists, live Q&A, and practical slide breakdowns.
  * **Executive Breakfast Link-Ups (In-Person):** Intimate, closed-door morning sessions in Lagos, Nairobi, and regional hubs for directors and corporate strategists.

### 3. Analysts Hangouts (Casual & High-Energy)
* **What It Is:** Relaxed evening mixers and weekend coffee chats designed to help analysts talk shop without corporate pressure.
* **Why Attend:** Unwind with fellow researchers, talk about real market stories behind the numbers, debate economic theories, and build authentic professional friendships.
* **Atmosphere:** Casual, collaborative, and fun. Drinks, coffee, and lively debates.

### 4. Special Interest Working Groups
* **What It Is:** Dedicated focus groups for specialists diving deep into specific sectors:
  * 🌾 **Agribusiness & Food Security Group:** Tracking crop yields, fertilizer supply, and rural freight.
  * 💵 **Macro & Currency Watch:** Tracking exchange rates, inflation, and sovereign fiscal policy.
  * 🛒 **Retail & Supply Chain Circle:** Analyzing neighborhood shops, FMCG distribution, and logistics.
  * ⚡ **Energy & Infrastructure Hub:** Discussing commercial solar, power tariffs, and mining.

## 7. Community Guidelines (Keeping Our Space High-Quality)
* 🤝 **Respect Everyone’s Time & Perspective:** We welcome diverse viewpoints, vigorous debates, and constructive critiques of data. Personal attacks or disrespectful behavior are never tolerated.
* 🚫 **Zero Spam & Hard Selling:** Our community is a place to learn, connect, and collaborate—not a classifieds board for unsolicited sales pitches.
* 🔒 **Respect Chatham House Rule:** At closed-door boardroom roundtables, participants are free to use the insights discussed, but neither the identity nor the affiliation of the speaker may be publicly quoted without explicit permission.
* 💡 **Facts and Evidence First:** Support claims with data, real observations, or transparent sources whenever possible.
