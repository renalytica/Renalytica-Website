## 1. Project Overview & Guiding Principle

Renalytica’s mission is **"Democratizing Direction"**—helping business owners, executives, and investors make confident decisions by turning complex economic numbers into clear, accessible market intelligence across agriculture, commerce, and the economy.

### Key Content Directive: Simple & Accessible Language
As guided by the project leadership, all website content has been written in **plain, clear, and accessible English**. 
* **Professional, Yet Plain:** It appeals directly to senior business executives, C-suite leaders, and investors without burying them in academic jargon, mathematical clutter, or marketing buzzwords.
* **Fast to Read & Actionable:** Every page emphasizes immediate practical takeaways, clear pricing, verified facts, and easy next steps.
