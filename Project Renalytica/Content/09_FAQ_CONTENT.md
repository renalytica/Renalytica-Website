## 4. Section 2: Licensing Tiers & Permitted Usage

### Q2.1: What is the difference between Single User, Departmental, and Global Enterprise licenses?
**Answer:** We structure our research licenses clearly to balance compliance with organizational collaboration:
* **Single User License ($2,500 USD standard):**
  * Granted to **one (1) designated individual** on up to two devices (e.g., work laptop and desktop).
  * Digital PDF only (read-only, printing disabled).
  * Strictly prohibited from being posted on internal company intranets, shared network drives, or distributed to colleagues.
* **Departmental License ($4,500 USD standard — Most Popular):**
  * Granted for up to **five (5) team members** within a single business unit or department (e.g., Corporate Strategy, Treasury, or Agribusiness M&A).
  * Includes the **Unlocked Excel Data Pack (.xlsx)** with full modeling rights.
  * Printable PDF rights for internal meeting packs and board papers.
  * Includes a **1-hour private briefing video call** with the lead economist.
* **Global Enterprise Site License ($7,500 USD standard):**
  * **Unlimited access** for all employees, directors, and subsidiaries of your corporate group worldwide.
  * Full authorization to host the report on internal enterprise document servers, SharePoint, and intranets.
  * Includes the Unlocked Excel Data Pack and Executive PowerPoint Deck.
  * Includes a **2-hour formal boardroom presentation** and executive Q&A delivered by our senior analysts.
  * 12 months of quarterly macroeconomic and data update addenda.

## 5. Section 3: Data Verification & Dual-Track Methodology

### Q3.1: How does Renalytica verify its market data and projections?
**Answer:** Every Renalytica publication is grounded in our proprietary **Dual-Track Verification Methodology**. We refuse to rely solely on recycled secondary web articles or unverified desktop extrapolations.
1. **Track 1: Primary On-The-Ground Enumeration:** We deploy field enumerators across 180+ major trading hubs, commercial ports, agricultural aggregation depots, and wholesale markets across 14 African economies. They capture daily farmgate commodity prices, truck haulage costs, retail sachet turnover, and physical inventory levels.
2. **Track 2: Authoritative Secondary Data Repositories:** Our quantitative desk simultaneously ingests official data from central banks, ministries of finance, national statistics agencies, multilateral agencies (World Bank, AfDB, FAO), and audited corporate filings.
3. **The Triangulation & Harmonization Engine:** Both data streams are mathematically cross-checked to eliminate anomalies, normalized against exchange rate distortions, and subjected to 10,000 Monte Carlo simulation runs before undergoing formal peer review by two senior economists.
