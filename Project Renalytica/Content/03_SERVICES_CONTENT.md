## 4. Service Pillar 2: Custom Research & Feasibility Studies (Bespoke Advisory)

### 4.1 Overview & Value Proposition
* **Service Headline:** **Tailored Research & Field Verification for Your Specific Project**
* **Who It’s For:** Multinationals entering new African markets, institutional private equity funds conducting commercial due diligence, commercial banks underwriting major industrial loans, and conglomerates planning large-scale manufacturing or outgrower infrastructure.
* **Core Value:** Off-the-shelf reports provide the broader industry landscape, but when you are committing tens of millions of dollars to a specific facility, corridor, or acquisition, you require bespoke empirical verification. We deploy dedicated field teams to uncover the exact on-the-ground reality behind official statistics.

### 4.2 Core Bespoke Practice Areas
1. **Commercial Due Diligence (CDD) for M&A & Private Equity:**
   * Independent verification of a target company’s real market share, customer retention, pricing power, and distribution health before deal execution.
   * Uncovering hidden customer churn, distributor inventory stuffing, and margin vulnerability.
2. **Market Entry Sizing & Regulatory Feasibility:**
   * Determining the exact Addressable Market (TAM / SAM / SOM) for a new product, packaged good, or financial service.
   * Evaluating licensing prerequisites, tariff structures, import clearance bottlenecks, and local content regulations.
3. **Supply Chain, Outgrower & Sourcing Audits:**
   * Mapping local raw material supplies within a 150-kilometer radius of a proposed factory site.
   * Evaluating smallholder farmer aggregation networks, post-harvest spoilage rates, road freight costs per ton-kilometer, and seasonal crop yields.
4. **Primary Consumer & Retail Channel Audits:**
   * High-frequency retail store audits across thousands of informal open-air market stalls and neighborhood kiosks.
   * Measuring real shelf availability, off-take velocity, wholesale cash margins, and brand perception against competing imported or local products.

### 4.3 Our 4-Phase Research Lifecycle & Timeline

```
PHASE 1: SCOPING & HYPOTHESIS (Week 1)

     ▼
```

### 4.4 Representative Case Studies
* **Case Study 1: Pan-African FMCG Sourcing Optimization**  
  * *The Client:* A leading global packaged foods conglomerate spending $40M annually on imported wheat and corn starch.  
  * *The Challenge:* Devaluation of local currencies doubled import landing costs; the board needed to determine if local cassava and maize outgrower schemes could reliably replace 40% of imports within 24 months.  
  * *Our Engagement:* Renalytica surveyed 420 commercial farms across 6 agricultural belts, analyzed processing capacities of 14 local mills, and stress-tested logistics costs.  
  * *The Result:* Identified a high-yield agricultural cluster in Central Nigeria and Western Ghana capable of fulfilling supply at a 26% cost reduction compared to imported grain, saving the client $7.8M in year one.
* **Case Study 2: Commercial Due Diligence on East African Cold-Chain Asset**  
  * *The Client:* A European Private Equity fund evaluating a $35M majority equity acquisition in a regional cold storage and refrigerated trucking operator.  
  * *The Challenge:* Target claimed a 42% market share with pharmaceutical and horticultural exporters, but historical financial statements were unaudited.  
  * *Our Engagement:* Our field enumerators conducted primary volume audits across Mombasa and Dar es Salaam port terminals and interviewed 65 top horticultural exporters under Chatham House rules.  
  * *The Result:* Discovered actual operational market share was 24% due to frequent fleet breakdowns and revealed an impending refrigerated warehouse development by a rival group. Enabled the PE fund to renegotiate valuation downward by $6.5M and structure protective performance earn-outs.
