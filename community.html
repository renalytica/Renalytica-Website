To maintain our status as Africa's definitive ground-truth benchmark, every fellow, auditor, and corporate subscriber adheres to four foundational governance pillars.

Participants are free to use information received in roundtables and working group discussions, but neither the identity nor the affiliation of speakers may be revealed.

Contributors who hold active trading positions or speculative contracts in commodities under census must disclose interests prior to submitting price telemetry.

Cryptographic Provenance

Every field observation is anchored by immutable GPS coordinates, device hashes, and time-stamped photo proofs to ensure zero synthetic data hallucination.

Replicable Methodology
