SECTION 02

Principles of Lawful Data Processing

In accordance with Section 24 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and Article 5 of the GDPR, all data processing undertaken by Renalytica adheres to the following foundational principles:

  1. Lawfulness, Fairness & Transparency: Personal data is collected through fair, transparent means with explicit legal justifications and clear disclosures to individuals.
  2. Purpose Limitation: Data is obtained strictly for specified, explicit, and legitimate commercial intelligence and research fulfillment purposes, and is not processed in ways incompatible with those aims.
  3. Data Minimization: We collect only the minimum amount of data strictly necessary to fulfill transactions, provide client support, and deliver research insights.
  4. Accuracy: We maintain reasonable operational measures to ensure personal information remains accurate, current, and verified.
  5. Storage Limitation: Information is retained only for as long as necessary to achieve the designated operational or statutory objectives.
  6. Integrity & Confidentiality: Data is secured against unauthorized access, accidental loss, destruction, or compromise using rigorous technical and organizational controls.

SECTION 06

Cookie Policy & Tracking Technologies

6.1 What Are Cookies? Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you browse websites. They help websites remember your device and store session preferences.

6.2 How Renalytica Uses Cookies:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for enabling site navigation, maintaining shopping cart contents, and keeping you securely authenticated in your subscription session.
  • Functional Cookies: Store your localized preferences, such as selected currency display (USD, NGN, GBP, EUR) and light/dark mode theme selection.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Collect anonymous, aggregate telemetry regarding which report categories are most frequently accessed, helping us optimize store navigation.

6.3 Cookie Governance & Control: You can modify your browser settings at any time to block, disable, or delete cookies. Note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may impair the functionality of the online shopping cart and digital download checkout.

SECTION 08

Information Security & Technical Safeguards

We implement institutional-grade technical, physical, and administrative safeguards to protect your personal and corporate information:

  • Transport Encryption: All web traffic, payment transactions, and file downloads are protected by 256-bit TLS (Transport Layer Security) encryption.
  • Storage Encryption: Stored databases, order records, and customer profiles are encrypted at rest using AES-256 cryptographic standards.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Access to internal order logs and client communication histories is restricted solely to authorized staff who require it for order fulfillment.
  • Periodic Vulnerability Audits: Our engineering team conducts regular vulnerability assessments, automated dependency scanning, and third-party code audits to safeguard against unauthorized intrusion.
SECTION 09

Data Retention & Secure Destruction Schedules

9.1 Retention Schedules:

  • Purchased Report Download Records: Retained for the active duration of your license plus three (3) years to facilitate license upgrades and file recovery requests.
  • Tax & Invoicing Documentation: Retained for seven (7) years in accordance with statutory Nigerian corporate taxation and accounting audit laws.
  • Marketing & Newsletter Subscriptions: Retained until you opt out or click "Unsubscribe". Upon unsubscribing, your email is immediately expunged from active mailing lists.
  • Technical Log Files: Server access and error logs are automatically pruned and deleted after ninety (90) days.

9.2 Secure Destruction: Upon expiration of applicable retention periods, digital records are permanently overwritten and purged using DoD-standard electronic shredding protocols, ensuring information cannot be retrieved or reconstructed.

SECTION 10

Your Legal Rights as a Data Subject

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you hold enforceable legal rights concerning your personal data:

  1. Right to Access (Subject Access Request): You have the right to request a complete copy of all personal data Renalytica holds regarding you and your organization.
  2. Right to Rectification: You have the right to demand the immediate correction of any inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated information.
  3. Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your personal data, provided retention is no longer mandated by applicable financial or corporate taxation laws.
  4. Right to Restrict Processing: You have the right to restrict our processing of your personal information under certain circumstances (e.g., while an accuracy dispute is being verified).
  5. Right to Data Portability: You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format (e.g., JSON or CSV).
  6. Right to Object: You have the right to object at any time to the processing of your personal data for direct marketing purposes.
  7. Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent (such as our email newsletter), you may withdraw your consent instantly by clicking the unsubscribe link or contacting our DPO.
How to Exercise Your Statutory Rights (30-Day SLA)

To exercise any of these rights, submit a written request to our Data Protection Officer at info@renalytica.com. We will verify your corporate identity and respond comprehensively within thirty (30) calendar days at zero cost to you.
