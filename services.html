Representative Case Studies

CASE STUDY 01 // AGRIBUSINESS & SUPPLY CHAIN

Pan-African FMCG Sourcing Optimization

The Client: A leading global packaged foods conglomerate spending $40M annually on imported wheat and corn starch.
The Challenge: Devaluation of local currencies doubled import landing costs; the board needed to determine if local cassava and maize outgrower schemes could reliably replace 40% of imports within 24 months.
Our Engagement: Renalytica surveyed 420 commercial farms across 6 agricultural belts, analyzed processing capacities of 14 local mills, and stress-tested road freight economics.
The Result: Identified a high-yield cluster in Central Nigeria and Western Ghana capable of fulfilling supply at a 26% cost reduction compared to imported grain, saving the client $7.8M in year one.
$7.8M Annual Raw Material Cost Savings

Achieved through domestic supply substitution with 420 surveyed commercial farms, lowering total procurement expenditure by 26% relative to imported grain.

CASE STUDY 02 // PRIVATE EQUITY CDD

Commercial Due Diligence on East African Cold-Chain Asset

The Client: A European Private Equity fund evaluating a $35M majority equity acquisition in a regional cold storage and refrigerated trucking operator.
The Challenge: Target claimed a 42% market share with pharmaceutical and horticultural exporters, but historical financial statements were unaudited.
Our Engagement: Our field enumerators conducted primary volume audits across Mombasa and Dar es Salaam port terminals and interviewed 65 top horticultural exporters under Chatham House rules.
The Result: Discovered actual operational market share was 24% due to fleet breakdowns and revealed an impending rival cold storage development. Enabled fund to renegotiate valuation downward by $6.5M.
-$6.5M Valuation Reduction Negotiated

Empirical terminal audits proved real market share was 24% (versus claimed 42%), protecting investor capital and establishing protective performance earn-outs.
